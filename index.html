How to play

  • Finish all 8 categories with 65 moves.
  • Put a category card on an empty field, then stack its word cards on it. The number on the category card says how many.
  • Every card you play costs a move – a wrong one too (it bounces back).
  • Tap face-down cards to turn them over (free). An emptied bottom field is a free field.
  • On a bottom field you can pile word cards of the same category onto the face-up word card. This run moves as a whole – for one move.
  • Park draw cards on Discard. A category card still alone on its field can go back there too (1 move). Draw pile empty? Flip the discard pile (free).

Drag a card or run, or tap it and then tap where it goes. Tap the draw card twice to discard it.

A new puzzle every day at midnight.
