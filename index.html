How to play

  • Draw a card and put it on a field.
  • Each field collects one hidden category — 4 cards completes it.
  • Doesn’t fit? It bounces back and counts as a mistake.
  • Park cards on Discard; when the draw pile runs out, flip the discard pile.
  • Finish all 6 categories.

Drag a card, or tap a field to play the highlighted card. Tap the Discard card to pick it instead; tap the draw card again to park it.

A new puzzle every day at midnight.
