How to play

  • Finish all 8 categories with 65 moves.
  • Put a category card on an empty field, then stack its word cards on it. The counter (e.g. 2/5) shows how many are on it and how many belong there.
  • Every card you play costs a move. Doesn’t fit? It bounces back – no move lost.
  • Tap face-down cards to turn them over (free). An emptied bottom field is a free field.
  • On a bottom field you can pile word cards onto a face-up card of their category – a word card or the category card itself (it completes right there). Such a run moves as a whole, for one move; with its category card only onto a free field.
  • Park draw cards on Discard. A category card still alone on its field can go back there too (1 move). Draw pile empty? Flip the discard pile (free).
  • Stuck? Tap a run on a bottom field, then ⤓: it slides under that pile’s face-down cards (2 moves) and you can turn the next card.

Drag a card or run, or tap it and then tap where it goes. Tap the draw card twice to discard it.

A new puzzle every day at midnight.
